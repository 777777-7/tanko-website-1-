About Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd

Malaysia's exclusive retail distributor for Tanko industrial storage — engineered in Taiwan since 1975, delivered and supported locally from Selangor.

Who we are

Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd (Company No. 756588-H) was incorporated in Malaysia on 15 December 2006, and has spent nearly two decades supplying industrial storage systems to Malaysian factories, workshops, laboratories, campuses and warehouses. Our range covers everything from medium-duty shelving through to heavy-duty racking, custom configurations and specialist storage — with our engineering team helping customers pick the right specification for the job rather than over-selling standard SKUs.

Our exclusive Tanko partnership

Primaxs is the exclusive Malaysia retail distributor for Tanko Enterprise Co., Ltd., a Taiwanese manufacturer that has been building industrial storage since 1975. Tanko's range is one of the deepest in the region — workbenches (Performance, Professional, Heavy Duty, Stainless Steel, Hexagonal), tool cabinets (Standard, Heavy-Duty, Mobile, CNC), lockers, modular workstations, mould racks, perforated boards, hanger racks, parts cabinets and more — with every product built for daily industrial use.

Being the exclusive Malaysia channel means we hold local stock for popular configurations, quote in Ringgit with delivery and warranty administered locally, and can source or configure any variant Tanko manufactures — without customers dealing with the freight, customs and cross-border support of a direct import.

Why buy through Primaxs

  • Local stock & fast delivery — popular models are stocked in Malaysia, with nationwide delivery across Peninsular and East Malaysia.
  • Real specification help — our team has physically handled the range and can steer you to the right size, top surface, drawer layout or holder interface before you commit.
  • Bulk & custom quotes — pricing that reflects real B2B quantities, plus configured orders (drawer counts, top materials, accessory kits) quoted directly.
  • Local warranty & support — claims and after-sales handled through our Selangor office, not a cross-border RMA queue.
  • No import overhead — freight, insurance, customs clearance and currency exposure are all absorbed in a single Ringgit quote.

Where we operate

Our office and inventory hub is in the Balakong Jaya industrial estate in Seri Kembangan, Selangor — inside the Klang Valley industrial belt, with delivery capability across Malaysia. We work with automotive workshops, injection-moulding plants, contract manufacturers, laboratories, universities, technical colleges and defence/facility maintenance teams.
