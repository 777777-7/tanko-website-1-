\---

name: seo-audit

description: Runs a full technical and on-page SEO scan on local website files.

\---



Perform a complete local SEO audit on the repository:

1\. Scan HTML/JSX/PHP templates for missing `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, and `<h1>` tags.

2\. Check that all `<img>` tags possess non-empty `alt` attributes.

3\. Verify the existence and formatting of `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` in the root/public directory.

4\. Check for JSON-LD schema scripts.

5\. Print a table detailing all missing metadata and broken heading structures.
