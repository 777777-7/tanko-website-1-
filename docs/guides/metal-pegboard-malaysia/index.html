Metal Pegboard in Malaysia: Sizes, Hooks, Prices and When Steel Beats Wood

By Wong Wei Ming, Business Development Manager · Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd · Published 3 October 2026

Most pegboards sold in Malaysia are made for homes: fibreboard, wood or plastic panels for a study wall or a craft corner. A workshop asks more of a board. Every time a spanner comes off at an angle the hook levers against the hole, dozens of times a shift, in a humid building. That is where a steel pegboard earns its price.

Direct answer: For a workshop, production line or store room, buy a painted steel pegboard and steel hooks. Choose #304 stainless where there is washdown, food contact or salt air. Wooden and plastic pegboards are fine for homes and light displays, but not for tools pulled off the board all day.

Metal vs wooden vs plastic pegboard

MaterialWhere it worksWhere it struggles
Wood or fibreboard (hardboard)Homes, studios, retail and decorative displaysHoles wear oval under daily hook loads; can swell in humid rooms
PlasticLight items, colour-coded home storagePanel flexes when heavier tools are pulled off at an angle
Painted steelWorkshops, production lines, maintenance stores, workbench back panelsNeeds proper wall fixing; heavier to install
#304 stainless steelFood and beverage, pharmaceutical, labs, coastal and wet areasCosts more than painted steel

Malaysia's year-round humidity is the quiet deciding factor. A board that absorbs moisture or rusts at a chipped hole will not hold its layout, and once outlines stop matching hooks, people stop using the board.

How to size a pegboard wall

  1. Collect the tools that belong at this station, and only those.
  2. Lay them out flat on the floor or a bench in the order they are used.
  3. Measure the area they cover, then add about a third for tools you will add later.
  4. Pick the board height first. 450mm boards suit the space above a workbench; 600mm boards suit open walls. Then pick widths, combining boards for long walls.
  5. Count the hooks by type from the layout, plus spares. This is the list we quote from.

Keep the most-used tools between waist and shoulder height. Heavy or rarely used items go low; nothing heavy goes above head height.

Wall, workbench or trolley?

Because the hole pitch is the same across the range, one set of hooks works in three places:

  • On the wall, as a tool wall or shadow board for a whole bay.
  • On a workbench, on the back panel or uprights, so the tools for that bench stay at that bench. See workbenches.
  • On a trolley or mobile cabinet: the panels on Tanko EKA-3M, EKB-3M, EGM-1 and ELA-1 units take the same hooks. See tool trolley vs tool chest vs tool cabinet.

Free-standing hanger racks carry pegboard and louvre panels where there is no usable wall, for example in the middle of an open production floor.

Installing on Malaysian walls

Most factory walls here are brick or concrete, which hold a steel board well with the right anchors. Two checks before drilling: make sure there is clearance behind the board so hooks seat fully through the holes, and make sure the fixings are rated for the full load of tools you plan to hang. On a plasterboard partition, fix into the frame rather than the board. Installation is included free in Selangor and Kuala Lumpur, so we handle this for most customers.

From pegboard to shadow board

A pegboard becomes a tool-control system when each tool gets a marked outline: a gap then shows at a glance what is missing. The five board colours help here, with a contrasting background behind dark tools, or one colour per zone. For layout rules and audit routines, read perforated boards and shadow boarding for tool control.

Frequently asked questions

Is a metal pegboard better than a wooden pegboard?

For a workshop or factory, yes. A steel board keeps its hole shape under repeated hook loads and does not absorb moisture. Wood and fibreboard are lighter and cheaper and suit homes and retail displays, but their holes wear under daily tool use and the board can swell in humid conditions.

How much weight can a pegboard hook hold?

It depends on the hook, not just the board. Tanko KP-3 metal hooks are rated 3–6kg depending on length and type, KP-7 stainless steel hooks 5kg, and KP-6 plastic hooks 2–4kg. The KP-17 shelf takes 50kg and the KP-4802 monitor arm 12kg. Total board load also depends on how the board is fixed to the wall.

What size pegboard should I buy?

Lay out the tools that belong at the station, measure the area they cover, and add about a third for growth. Tanko steel boards come 450mm high in widths of 900, 1,200, 1,500, 1,800 and 2,100mm, and 600mm high in widths of 1,200, 1,500, 1,800 and 2,100mm.

How much does a metal pegboard cost in Malaysia?

Tanko steel pegboard guide prices run from RM168.70 for the W900 × H450mm KQ-306A to RM494.05 for the W2100 × H600mm KQ-414A. The #304 stainless KQ-306AS is RM397.65. Hooks and holders are extra.

Will other brands’ hooks fit a Tanko pegboard?

Not reliably. Hole size and hole pitch vary between brands. Tanko keeps one hole pitch across its whole range, so Tanko hooks fit every Tanko board, workbench upright and trolley panel. If you have existing hooks, send us a photo with a ruler and we will check.

Malaysia compliance notes

Industrial storage specified for Malaysian factories, workshops and campuses is typically governed by the Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 (OSHA) for aisle clearance and load ratings, the Factories and Machinery Act 1967 for storage adjacent to plant, and the local Uniform Building By-Laws (UBBL) for fire egress spacing. Malaysian standards (MS series) published by Department of Standards Malaysia apply to specific steel-furniture ratings. Enforcement sits with DOSH / JKKP for factory sites and Bomba for fire-code clearance. Confirm compartment weight limits and aisle widths with your facility's safety officer before deployment — Primaxs can supply Tanko's product data sheets for compliance sign-off on request.
