05 INDUSTRIAL STORAGE MALAYSIA

Workbenches, tool cabinets & storage
for Malaysian industry.

Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd is the exclusive Malaysia distributor for Tanko Enterprise Co., Ltd. — the Taiwan industrial storage manufacturer established in 1975. Our catalogue covers industrial workbenches, modular workstations, tool cabinets, CNC tool storage, steel lockers, parts cabinets, warehouse racking and perforated boards — all built to Taiwanese quality standards and supported locally from our Selangor office.

Malaysian factories, workshops and facilities choose Tanko because the range is engineered for real shop-floor conditions. Heavy-duty workbenches handle fabrication, assembly and maintenance tasks with load ratings that match production demands. Tool cabinets and roller cabinets keep expensive hand tools secure and organised across shifts. CNC tool storage protects BT, HSK and ISO tool holders from chips and moisture in machining shops across Selangor, Johor and Penang.

Whether you are outfitting a new production line in Klang, upgrading a maintenance workshop in Johor Bahru, or equipping a technical training centre in Kuching, Primaxs provides full specification support, Ringgit quotations, Malaysia stock on popular models and nationwide delivery. Our team has physically handled the full Tanko range — we help you select the right configuration, drawer layout and top material for your application, not just sell you a catalogue number.

We serve manufacturing plants, automotive workshops, CNC machining shops, laboratories and cleanrooms, schools and campuses, electronics and EMS facilities, and food and pharmaceutical production across Malaysia. Every product comes with the Tanko manufacturer warranty, administered locally through our office — no cross-border RMA delays.

Browse the full range above, or send us your model list for a fast quotation with pricing, lead time and delivery options. We also recommend reading our industrial storage guides for practical advice on specifying workbenches, tool cabinets and storage systems for Malaysian conditions.

06 FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions Malaysian buyers
ask about Tanko storage.

Is Primaxs the official Tanko distributor in Malaysia?

Yes. Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd is the exclusive retail distributor for Tanko Enterprise Co., Ltd. in Malaysia. We have represented the brand since 2006 and hold Malaysia stock for popular configurations. Every Tanko product purchased through Primaxs is covered under the manufacturer warranty with claims administered locally through our Selangor office.

How long is the delivery time for Tanko products in Malaysia?

Popular models held in our Selangor warehouse can be delivered within 3–7 working days to KL, Selangor, Johor, Penang and other Malaysian states. Configured or less common models may require 2–4 weeks for Taiwan production and sea freight. When you send us your model list, we confirm current stock status and lead time for each item before you order.

Can I get a quotation in Ringgit (MYR)?

Yes. All quotations from Primaxs are in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR). We provide unit prices, bulk pricing for quantity orders, and delivery charges separately. Reference prices on product pages are guide prices — the final quotation depends on configuration, quantity and delivery location. Send us your list for an accurate quote.

What industries use Tanko industrial storage in Malaysia?

Tanko products are used across Malaysian manufacturing, automotive repair, CNC machining, electronics assembly, food processing, pharmaceutical production, laboratories, schools and technical training centres. Our workbenches serve fabrication and assembly lines; tool cabinets equip automotive and MRO workshops; CNC tool storage supports precision machining; and lockers serve factories, campuses and public facilities nationwide.

Do you offer bulk or project pricing for factories?

Yes. We offer competitive B2B bulk pricing for factory outfitting, production line setup and facility upgrade projects across Malaysia. For large orders, we can also provide layout advice, phased delivery and on-site coordination. Contact us with your project requirements and quantities for a tailored quotation.

How does the warranty work for Tanko products in Malaysia?

All Tanko products supplied through Primaxs are covered under the Taiwan manufacturer warranty. If you encounter a manufacturing defect, contact our office with the product details and photos — we handle the warranty claim locally, avoiding the need to ship items back to Taiwan. We also provide spare parts and after-sales support for the full range.

Can Tanko workbenches and cabinets be customised?

Tanko offers configurable options across many ranges — workbench tops (laminate, wood, rubber, stainless steel), drawer counts, pegboard panels, overhead lighting and power sockets. Modular workstations can be assembled in multiple configurations. Tell us your application and we will recommend the right combination from the available Tanko options.
