About the WBT-5203F4A Professional Workbench — Drawers

The Professional Workbench — Drawers (WBT-5203F4A) is a heavy-duty industrial workbench engineered for continuous shop-floor use in Malaysian factories, fabrication workshops and maintenance departments. Built by Tanko Enterprise in Taiwan and distributed exclusively in Malaysia by Primaxs Marketing, this workbench features a Laminate top(F) top measuring W1500xD750xH1400mm — providing a stable, flat work surface for assembly, inspection, repair and light fabrication tasks.

Designed for the demands of Malaysian industry, the WBT-5203F4A workbench supports high-load applications with its reinforced steel frame and level-adjustable feet. Whether you are outfitting a production line in Penang, a maintenance bay in Johor or a technical training facility in KL, this Tanko workbench delivers the durability and ergonomics that B2B buyers expect. Popular configurations add drawer units, pegboards and overhead lighting — all configurable through Primaxs.

Primaxs Marketing holds Malaysia stock for popular workbench models and provides local warranty support, Ringgit quotations and nationwide delivery. Send us your model list and quantities for a fast quotation with lead time and freight options.
