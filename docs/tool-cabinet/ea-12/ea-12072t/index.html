About the EA-12072T Tool Cabinet - H1200 — Heavy Duty

The Tool Cabinet - H1200 — Heavy Duty (EA-12072T) is a professional tool storage cabinet designed for automotive workshops, MRO departments and manufacturing facilities across Malaysia. Manufactured by Tanko Enterprise in Taiwan and supplied by Primaxs Marketing — the exclusive Malaysia distributor — this tool cabinet offers secure, organised storage for hand tools, power tools and workshop consumables.

With dimensions of None and a None construction, the EA-12072T tool cabinet combines heavy-duty build quality with smart drawer organisation. Each drawer runs on precision slides rated for daily industrial use, while the central locking system keeps valuable tools secure across shifts. Mobile variants include heavy-duty casters for bay-side mobility in automotive and fabrication environments.

Primaxs Marketing stocks popular tool cabinet configurations in Malaysia and offers local warranty administration, Ringgit pricing and delivery to KL, Selangor, Johor, Penang and nationwide. Contact us with your required SKUs and quantities for a competitive B2B quotation.
