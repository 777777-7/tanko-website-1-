Industrial Storage for Automotive Workshops

By Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd · Exclusive Malaysia distributor for Tanko · Updated August 2026

Automotive workshops — from independent service centres to franchise dealerships across Malaysia — depend on fast, organised tool access. A technician who spends five minutes per job searching for tools loses hours per week. Tanko's automotive storage range — mobile tool trolleys, heavy-duty workbenches, tyre racks and steel lockers — is engineered to put every tool within reach and keep bays productive.

The automotive workshop storage challenge

Malaysian automotive workshops face specific storage pressures: multiple technicians sharing tools, expensive speciality tools that walk off, oil and coolant on every surface, and limited bay space. The right storage system addresses all four: mobile trolleys follow the technician to the bay, central locking secures tools, steel construction resists oil, and compact footprints maximise bay space.

Tool trolleys: the heart of the bay

A rolling tool trolley is the single most important storage investment for an automotive workshop. Tanko's tool trolleys feature full-extension ball-bearing slides (rated for daily industrial use), central locking with one key, and a range of drawer depths to separate hand tools, sockets and diagnostic equipment. Many Malaysian workshops equip each bay with one trolley and add a top chest for overflow — a setup that keeps tools mobile and secure.

Workbenches for tyre, service and repair bays

Service bays need a strong work surface for parts disassembly, inspection and minor fabrication. A rubber-top workbench absorbs impact and protects parts; a steel-top bench handles heavier fabrication and welding. In tyre shops, dedicated tyre and wheel storage racks keep inventory organised and off the floor. Tanko's workbench range covers both scenarios, with duty ratings matched to the work.

Shadow boarding & tool control

The fastest way to reduce tool loss in a Malaysian workshop is shadow boarding — mounting frequently used hand tools on a perforated board with foam outlines. A missing tool leaves an empty shadow, making the problem visible at a glance. This is especially valuable in shared workshops where tools cross bays. Tanko's perforated boards and full range of hooks, hangers and holders make shadow boarding easy to implement.

Lockers for technicians

Shift-based workshops need secure personal storage for technicians' belongings and PPE. Steel lockers with combination locks remove the key-management burden — no lost keys, no rekeying costs. Tanko's locker range offers single- and multi-compartment configurations with ventilation, important in Malaysia's humidity where uniforms and PPE need airflow.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best tool trolley for an automotive workshop in Malaysia?

A 6-8 drawer rolling tool trolley with full-extension ball-bearing slides and central locking is the standard for Malaysian automotive bays. Match drawer depths to your tool set — shallow for hand tools, deeper for sockets and diagnostic gear.

How do I prevent tools from going missing in a shared workshop?

Shadow boarding on perforated boards is the most effective method — every tool has a marked outline, so a missing tool is instantly visible. Pair this with central-locking trolleys assigned to each bay or technician.

Rubber or steel top for an automotive workbench?

Rubber tops suit service and repair bays where parts are handled and impact absorption matters. Steel tops suit fabrication, welding and heavy abuse. Many workshops use both — rubber in service bays, steel in fabrication areas.
