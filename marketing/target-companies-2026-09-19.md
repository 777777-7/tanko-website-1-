## The gap, stated plainly

**I have company names. I do not have named people or verified email addresses,
and that is the part that decides whether any of this works.**

Sending to `info@` or `enquiry@` at these companies is the failure mode I described
in the [outreach playbook](outreach-email-playbook.md) — shared inboxes are where
supplier mail dies. Guessing email patterns and sending in volume is worse: bounced
mail damages the sending reputation of `sales@storagesystem.my`, which is the
address you also quote from.

**The next step is per-company work, not a bigger list:**

1. **LinkedIn** — search each company plus "facilities", "maintenance",
   "production" or "engineering" plus Malaysia. Get the person's actual name.
2. **Work out the email pattern** from any published address at that company.
3. **Phone the switchboard** where LinkedIn gives nothing. In Malaysia this works
   better than it should — ask for the maintenance or facilities manager by role
   and you will often be put through or given a name.

**Twenty named people beats two hundred company names.**
