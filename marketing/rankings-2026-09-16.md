## What moved, honestly

Four terms slipped a place or three between 15 and 16 September:

- **perforated board malaysia** — was #1 *and* #2, now #2. johorhardware.com.my
  took the top spot.
- **document cabinet a4 tray malaysia** — was #1, now #2 behind felton.com.my.
- **mould rack malaysia** — #4 to #5.
- **heavy duty steel workbench malaysia** — #4 to #7, the largest single drop.

Nothing slipped *off*, and none of it correlates with a technical fault: every
page checked returns 200, `index, follow`, with a self-referencing canonical.

## Two findings that change what we should chase

**"warehouse storage malaysia" is the wrong industry.** It shows 97 impressions
in Search Console, and it looked like an opportunity. The actual results are
Crown Workspace, Asian Tigers and Rhenus — relocation and third-party
*warehousing services*. Nobody searching it wants to buy a rack. Those
impressions will never convert and should be ignored rather than optimised for.

**"heavy duty tools cabinet" is dominated by marketplaces** — RS Online,
Knight Auto, Shopee. That is a price-comparison search, not a specification
search, and it is not where a distributor with published load ratings wins.

## Caveat on this table

Day-on-day positions move for reasons that have nothing to do with the site.
A single check on one day from one browser is a sample, not a trend — and these
searches ran from a browser signed into a Google account, so some
personalisation survives even with `gl=my` forced. Treat single-place moves as
noise; the three-place workbench drop is worth re-checking in a week before
acting on it.

Nine guide titles were changed on 16 September to stop them competing with the
product pages. Google had **not** recrawled them at the time of this check --
the perforated board guide still showed its old title in results -- so none of
the movement above can be attributed to that change yet. That is precisely why
this table exists: it is the before-picture.
