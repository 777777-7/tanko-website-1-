## 4. THE STRATEGIC CALL: win workbench, not tool cabinet

Three reasons, and they all point the same way.

1. **Distance.** Workbench queries sit at 10–30. Tool cabinet queries sit at
   40–90. One of those is reachable this quarter.
2. **Depth.** Workbenches are **893 of 1,761 SKUs** — over half the catalogue,
   and by far the deepest range. Eight sub-families, every top material, every
   duty rating. That is a genuine authority claim.
3. **Competition.** "Tool cabinet" in Malaysia means competing with tool
   *retailers* — Knight Auto, Mr. Mark, Toptul, SP Tools — who sell boxes and
   trolleys at consumer prices and have years of domain age. "Industrial
   workbench" means competing with a handful of specialists, and Tanko's range
   is deeper than most of them.

**Concentrate everything on workbench. Let tool cabinet come later, on the back
of the authority workbench earns.**

advantage, it is a platform advantage. It is also why claiming the NEWPAGES
listing matters more than it sounds.

## 6. The honest answer about position 44

On-page work on this site is close to finished:

- SEO audit **49/50**
- **0** broken links, image sources, canonicals or hreflang
- Product, ProductGroup, FAQ, LocalBusiness and Breadcrumb schema throughout
- 28 guides, 11 in Bahasa Malaysia — almost no competitor does this
- AI answer engines explicitly allowed
- **Every page already links to every important page** — internal linking is
  saturated at 1,846 links to each key target. There is nothing left to gain there.

None of that moves "tool cabinet malaysia" from 44 to 8. What moves it is
**authority**, and authority in this vertical is built from exactly three things:

1. **Reviews.** 3 on Google, two of which say nothing. This is still the number
   one item on the list.
2. **Citations.** NEWPAGES (unclaimed, wrong address), Yellow Pages, BusinessList,
   MATRADE. The competitors on page one are on all of them.
3. **Time and links.** 33 clicks a month buys very little crawl budget, which is
   also why 1,228 pages sit unindexed.

Anyone promising to fix position 44 with meta tags is selling something.

## 7. What was changed today

**Product images in all 27 guides.** Every guide previously carried exactly one
`<img>`, and it was the Primaxs logo — meaning **zero presence in Google Images**.

54 images added, two per guide, with keyword-bearing alt text, useful captions,
responsive `srcset` and explicit dimensions. Bahasa guides got Bahasa alt text.

This matters because **the guides are what actually rank** — "tool display board"
at 6.0 with 28.6% CTR, "perforated board" at 7.8 with 20%. They were the
strongest pages on the site and invisible in image search. Image results are a
second, separate way onto page one for exactly the queries already working.
