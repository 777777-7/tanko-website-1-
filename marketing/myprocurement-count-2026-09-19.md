## The headline

**Do not pay the RM450 yet.** Not because government work is worthless, but
because the feed under your codes is thin for *your* product, and one live job
suggests a better reason to register than the one we assumed.

### And the prices are mostly hidden

Only **24% publish a Harga Indikatif Jabatan**. Three quarters say *Tiada
Maklumat*. So you cannot size this market from the advertisements even if the
volume were there — you would be bidding blind most of the time.

## The one live job worth your attention

> **CHEMICAL STORAGE CABINET (ACID DAN FLAMMABLE)**
> Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Fakulti Sains Gunaan, **Shah Alam, Selangor**
> Membekal, menghantar, memasang dan mengujilari
> **Closes 28 September 2026** · Harga indikatif: not published

This matters for three reasons:

1. **It is in Selangor** — your free delivery and free installation zone.
2. **It is exactly the Chung Fu product line** you were investigating. This is the
   first hard evidence that Malaysian government demand for FM-type flammable and
   acid storage cabinets is real and not hypothetical.
3. **You cannot bid it.** It closes in nine days and MOF registration does not
   happen in nine days.

**That is the actual argument for registering: not the volume, but being ready
when the right job appears.** You just watched one go past.

### But be honest about how rare it is

I searched the entire KEPUTUSAN archive for "STORAGE CABINET" and for
"FLAMMABLE". **Zero results, both terms.** So this is not a steady stream. It is
an occasional job. Register for optionality, not for a pipeline.

### This answers the question you asked me on Thursday

You asked whether, as an importer, they would really choose you, or whether they
need a local manufacturer. **The names answer it, and the answer is mixed:**

- **Timber Dynamics, Meranti Marshal, Homestead Wood, Kabimas Manufacturing,
  Azas Rekalogam, B.TIS Manufacturer** — manufacturers, and mostly *wood*
  manufacturers. Government furniture demand is largely timber, which is not your
  product at all.
- **But Raudah Marketing & Supplies** — a trading company — is the single most
  frequent winner on the list. **AXEC Services & Trading** won "Pembekalan
  Pelbagai Jenis Kabinet" for TLDM at RM43,547.

**So traders do win. You are not excluded for being an importer.** What you are
mostly excluded from is the wood furniture that makes up the bulk of the volume.

## What I would do

**Skip the RM450 this month.** The evidence does not support it as a pipeline.

**Instead, spend zero ringgit and do this:**

1. **Watch the feed weekly** under `020101` and `060501` — it is free and needs no
   registration. Fifteen minutes. If two or three more chemical-cabinet or
   steel-locker jobs appear over the next two months, register then, with a real
   reason.
2. **Chase the military channel directly.** ATM, TLDM and the matriculation and
   training colleges are the repeat buyers of steel lockers and cabinets in this
   data. That is a relationship, not a portal.
3. **Treat the UiTM job as a signal, not a loss.** It tells you the Chung Fu line
   has government demand in your own state. That strengthens the case for the
   Chung Fu approach — which is a supplier decision, not a procurement one.

**The private new-plant list is still the better use of the same hours.** NXP
Petaling Jaya has no tender, no Bumiputera scoring, no price-only evaluation, and
it is in Selangor. Nothing in today's numbers changes that ranking.
