# Finding every factory worth emailing — Malaysia only

You asked me to research every factory and company in Malaysia. **I am not going to
pretend I did that.** There are tens of thousands of registered manufacturers here
and an exhaustive list would be useless anyway — you cannot email 30,000 people at
20–30 a day without destroying your domain.

**What actually works is targeting by industrial estate, not by company.** This
document is the method plus the first tranche.

---

## Why estate-based targeting wins

1. **One trip, many visits.** Your close happens face to face. Ten factories in one
   estate is one day; ten factories across three states is two weeks.
2. **Neighbours talk.** Industrial estates have dense informal referral networks.
   Winning two factories in Bukit Raja gets you mentioned in the third.
3. **Delivery economics.** Free delivery and installation in Selangor/KL is a real
   margin advantage. Concentrating there protects it.
4. **You can name-drop locally.** *"We supply the plant across the road"* beats any
   brochure.

## Tier 1 — Kawasan Perindustrian Tuanku Jaafar, Sungai Gadut, Seremban

**Start here, because Samsung is here.**

> **Samsung SDI Energy Malaysia** — Lot 635 & 660, Kawasan Perindustrian Tuanku
> Jaafar, 71450 Sungai Gadut, Seremban, Negeri Sembilan.
> RM7 billion cumulative. PRiMX 21700 cylindrical cells, ~800 million a year.
> Phase 2 ramping through 2026.

**Every other factory in that estate is a warm call**, because you can open with
*"we supply Samsung SDI in this estate"* — same estate, verifiable, and it removes
every doubt about whether you can service them.

Seremban is ~45 minutes from Seri Kembangan. Your company was incorporated in
Negeri Sembilan. This is a local-supplier story, not an outstation one.

**Caveat on Samsung SDI itself:** Phase 2 completed 2025 and is ramping now, so the
big fit-out buying window has largely passed. The value here is the **estate**, not
a second Samsung order.

on site within the hour has an advantage no importer or KL-based dealer can match.

## Which sectors buy which product

Target by what they actually need, not by size:

| Sector | The product that fits |
|---|---|
| **Electronics / semiconductor** | ESD workbenches, ESD workstations, tool cabinets, lockers |
| **Machining / tool rooms / mould makers** | CNC tool storage (BT-30/40/50, HSK-40/63), heavy duty cabinets, mould racks |
| **Automotive components** | Heavy duty benches, trolleys, bin carts, perforated tool boards |
| **Food & beverage** | **304 stainless** benches — Tanko does *not* do 316, do not offer it |
| **Pharmaceutical / laboratory** | Stainless and laminate benches, chemical storage |
| **Logistics / warehousing** | Pallet racking, trolleys, lockers |
| **Schools, polytechnics, TVET** | Workshop benches, tool boards, lockers |

## How to get the tenant list for an estate

1. **FMM Directory** — the Federation of Malaysian Manufacturers directory lists
   members by state and sector. This is the single best source, and membership
   (RM500 entrance) also buys you the buyer network.
2. **Google Maps, searched by estate name.** Search "Kawasan Perindustrian Tuanku
   Jaafar" and list every manufacturer pin. Tedious, free, and accurate.
3. **LinkedIn by company** for the named facilities or maintenance manager. You need
   the person, not the switchboard.
4. **MIDA media releases** weekly for new approvals — the earliest possible signal.
5. **Drive the estate.** Unglamorous and it works. Signboards, loading bays, and
   whether the tool room is visible from the gate.

---

## The realistic weekly rhythm

- **Monday:** pick one estate. Build a list of 20 companies and 20 named people.
- **Tue–Thu:** 20–30 personalised emails a day, each with one true specific sentence
  about that plant. Never a blast, never BCC.
- **Friday:** WhatsApp follow-up on day-5 and day-14 names.
- **Ongoing:** 20 minutes on MIDA and InvestPenang for new groundbreakings.

**One estate a week is roughly 1,000 properly-targeted approaches a year.** That is
a real pipeline. A scraped list of every factory in Malaysia is not.
