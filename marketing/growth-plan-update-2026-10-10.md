## 2. What changed outside (researched 10 Oct 2026)

- **Budget 2027 was tabled on 9 Oct 2026.** RM8 billion for TVET across ministries; RM800
  million in the Higher Education budget for infrastructure and replacing outdated equipment;
  2027 declared the Year of SMEs. Training institutes and polytechnic workshops will be equipping.
  Sources: [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/09/budget-2027-rm8b-for-tvet-30000-new-job-opportunities-via-bakat-madani/238487),
  [Rakyat Post](https://www.therakyatpost.com/news/2026/10/09/budget-2027-education-gets-nearly-rm69-billion-auku-to-be-replaced-ptptn-repayment-relief-for-low-income-borrowers/),
  [Life News Agency](https://lifenewsagency.com/budget-2027-2027-declared-the-year-of-smes-malaysia/).
- **New plants are being fitted out now.** RM218.5 billion of approved investment in 1H 2026; 973
  manufacturing projects (+88%); Penang RM17.3 billion in manufacturing (+38%).
  Sources: [MIDA](https://www.mida.gov.my/media-release/malaysia-secures-rm218-5-billion-in-approved-investments-in-1h-2026-with-domestic-investment-in-manufacturing-up-23/),
  [The Edge](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/816173), [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/09/03/penang-manufacturing-investments-jump-38-to-rm173bil-in-1h2026).
  New named projects are added to `research-oct-2026/leads-and-partners.md` (section "Added 10 Oct").
- **Search is moving into AI Overviews / AI Mode.** Google says both draw on the normal index.
  Pages that get cited answer one question directly, carry original data and cite sources.
  Our guides already do this; keep the answer-first callout and the sources block on every
  new page. AI Mode appears on our own SERPs ("perforated board malaysia", 10 Oct).
- **Social formats.** LinkedIn rewards substantive comment threads and penalises links in the
  post body (link goes in the first comment, as we already do). Facebook organic reach is low
  for link posts; native video and images do better; groups still work. (Third-party sources,
  directional only.)

### B. New research-led page: new-factory fit-out checklist (this week, site)

plan; the 3 Oct MyProcurement list already had a Jabatan Penjara PEKA TVET workshop-equipment
quotation that we could not bid on). So: aim the page at private and HRD Corp-registered training
providers and corporate training centres first, and **register MOF before Budget 2027 money is
spent**.
