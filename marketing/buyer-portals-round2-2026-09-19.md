# The portals that actually carry orders — 19 September 2026, round 2

You asked for factories and companies and the portals they buy through. The useful
finding is not a longer list of companies. It is this:

**Malaysian corporates do not each run their own procurement system. They rent a
handful of shared platforms. Register once on the platform and you are visible to
every buyer on it.**

That is the difference between chasing 50 companies one at a time and doing four
registrations.

---

## The big one: PROCUREHERE

`app.procurehere.com` — built by **PRIVASIA Sdn Bhd**. This is the platform that
answers the Malaysia Airports question I could not solve this morning.

**MAHB's website has no working procurement page — because their tenders are on
Procurehere.** Their toner and cartridge supply tender and several consultancy RFIs
are published there under Malaysia Airports Holdings Berhad.

**Buyers confirmed on it today:**

| Buyer | Public events page |
|---|---|
| **Indah Water Konsortium** | `app.procurehere.com/publicEvents/iwk` — live, has an open RFP |
| **FELCRA Berhad** | `app.procurehere.com/publicEvents/felcra` — live events confirmed |
| **Malaysia Airports (MAHB)** | Events indexed under the platform; no public slug found |
| **Boustead Plantations** | Referenced as a public event feed |

**You can browse every public event without an account, for free.** Each buyer has
its own `/publicEvents/<slug>` page listing reference number, event type (RFI / RFP
/ RFQ / RFT / RFA / PO), participation fee, dates and whether there is a site
visit.

**Register:** `app.procurehere.com/suppliersubscription/supplierCheckout`

**Caution — I could not confirm the price.** The URL says "suppliersubscription"
and "Checkout", which points to a paid subscription, but the form asks for company
details before showing any figure. **Browse the free public events first and see
whether anything in your categories actually appears.** Do not subscribe on the
assumption that it will.

## Free registrations — do these first, they cost nothing

### Air Selangor — the best fit on this page

**`airselangor.com/partners/vendors/registration`**

Largest water services provider in Malaysia, serving **Selangor, Kuala Lumpur and
Putrajaya** — which is precisely your free delivery and free installation zone.
Treatment plants, pump stations and depots, all with workshops.

The form is short and self-service: company registration number, company name,
certified supporting documents, declaration, submit. **No fee is mentioned.**

Two things to know before you start:
- You must accept their Terms of Use and sign a **Declaration** binding the
  company. **That is yours to click, not mine.**
- One clause reads: *"I/we hereby allow representative(s) from Air Selangor to
  visit the premises of the company to verify any related document(s)."* A premises
  visit is possible. Your Seri Kembangan warehouse is an asset here, not a problem.

### Indah Water Konsortium — free, verified from their own page

**`iwk.com.my/contractor-registration`**

Their page states it plainly: *"The registration form is provided free of charge
and no registration fees are imposed for normal registration."* Registration lasts
**5 years**, renewable 60 days before expiry.

Only registered contractors and suppliers can participate in the eProcurement
tenders. IWK is owned by **Minister of Finance Incorporated** and runs the national
sewerage system — treatment plants and depots nationwide.

Their published requirements mention CIDB, **MoF** and SPAN licences *depending on
the category of work*. Sewerage works need SPAN; supplying goods should not.

### Prasarana — free Ariba self-registration

**`s1.ariba.com/Sourcing/Main/ad/selfRegistration?realm=prasarana`**

Covered in the earlier note. Bus and rail depots. No MOF requirement stated.

## Paid or gated

| Buyer | Route | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **TNB** | `srm.tnb.com.my/ros` | **RM50** per submission, **and a valid MOF certificate** |
| **Telekom Malaysia** | `tm.com.my/business-with-tm/registration` | **Registration fee payable** online or at a Unifi Store kiosk. Runs ProDigy and SAP Ariba. Support: `srm@tm.com.my` |
| **PETRONAS** | `licensing.petronas.com/vendor/login` | Take **Registration**, not License. ~10 working days |
| **Lapasar** | `mall.lapasar.com/become-a-supplier#apply` | Free to join, but read the penalty clause first — see the Lapasar note |

TM's process is four steps and they claim onboarding "within a few days". They also
run a **Bumiputera Vendor Development Program** as a separate track, which is not
your route.

leverage is.

## What I would actually do, in order

1. **Browse Procurehere's free public events** — IWK and FELCRA today. Fifteen
   minutes. See whether storage, furniture or workshop equipment ever appears. That
   decides whether the subscription is worth anything.
2. **Air Selangor registration** — free, self-service, and it is your home state.
3. **IWK registration** — free, five years, confirmed in writing on their page.
4. **Prasarana Ariba** — free.
5. Then the paid ones: MOF → TNB → PETRONAS.

**Steps 1 to 4 cost nothing and can all be done this weekend.**
