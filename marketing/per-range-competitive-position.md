## The five we win

Nobody in Malaysia has built a serious page for these. We did, so we own them:

- **Perforated boards** — #1 *and* #2. The nearest competitor is a Johor
  hardware shop selling hardboard pegboard.
- **CNC tool storage** — #1, plus two more results in the top ten. Only
  `jteasia.com` is a real industrial rival here.
- **Parts bins**, **hanger racks**, **document cabinets** — all #1, all against
  general hardware and stationery suppliers rather than specialists.

Nothing to fix. Keep publishing and hold them.

## The two we place in

**Mould racks — #4.** Above us: `yesdisplay.com.my`, `ttf.com.my`,
`mega-rack.my`. `mega-rack.my` is a racking specialist, so #1 is a genuine
fight, but #4 on a low-competition term is a winnable gap.

**Workbenches — #4** on "heavy duty steel workbench malaysia factory". Above us:
`newtech.com.my`, `smtsystems.com`, `cebsteel-industrial.com` — all Malaysian
fabricators who *manufacture* benches. We are a distributor. Being #4 against
three manufacturers on their own term is respectable.

Worth noting: this is our **largest range, 893 SKUs**, and it is not in the top
three for its own category term. The biggest upside on the whole site sits here.

## The three we are genuinely losing

### Tool cabinets — position 43
The one we already knew. 110 impressions a month, zero clicks. Page one is
Knight Auto, Toptul, Sui U, JTE — established suppliers with years of links.
**This is the authority problem, not a page problem.** Our page is technically
better than all of them; theirs are older and better linked. Nothing on-page
fixes this; it needs the Tanko link, the NEWPAGES microsite and time.

### Modular workstations — absent
Two different searches, absent from both, and for two different reasons:

- *"modular workstation malaysia"* returns **office furniture** — zenpro,
  majuhome, brownbox, uaoffice. In Malaysia that phrase means office cubicles,
  not industrial assembly stations. **We are not losing this; we are aiming at
  the wrong words.**
- *"assembly workstation industrial malaysia"* returns SMT Systems, Newtech,
  Artsystem — **systems integrators who build production lines**. They sell the
  line; the bench is incidental. Different business, deeper authority.

**What to do:** stop chasing "workstation". Target what our buyer actually
types — *ESD assembly bench*, *production line bench*, *packing station* — which
is the language already used in the guides.

### Steel lockers — absent
Both *"steel locker malaysia"* and *"wallet locker factory malaysia"* are owned
by office and school furniture suppliers: officepro, goodview, Asiastar, SWH,
Sweethome, GY. Lockers are their **entire business**; ours is 20 SKUs.

**Honest assessment:** we will not win the generic locker term and should not
spend on trying. The defensible corner is the one they do not serve — *wallet
lockers at a cleanroom gowning point*, *per-compartment ventilation for
Malaysian humidity*, *lockers specified for a factory rather than a school*.
Narrow, low volume, high fit.

## What this changes

The earlier conclusion — *"position 43, it is all an authority problem"* — was
drawn from a single head term and was **too pessimistic**. Across the catalogue
we already hold #1 in five ranges.

The picture is sharper now:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Win and hold** | perforated boards, CNC storage, parts bins, hanger racks, document cabinets |
| **Push — biggest upside** | **workbenches** (893 SKUs sitting at #4) and mould racks (#4) |
| **Needs links and time** | tool cabinets — nothing on-page will move position 43 |
| **Re-target, don't fight** | workstations — the head term means office furniture here |
| **Accept the niche** | lockers — cede the generic term, own the factory-specific corner |

The ranges where we are absent are not ranges where we are beaten on quality.
Two of the three are cases of aiming at a phrase whose searchers want something
else.
