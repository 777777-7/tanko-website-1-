
**Timeline posts are irreversible** — NEWPAGES states the points are
non-refundable once posted. So the four Timeline slots go to the four products
aimed at the best striking-distance keywords, not to the four most expensive
products.

## The description formula

Every description follows the same five moves. It is long enough to rank and
specific enough to be worth reading.

1. **What it is, who supplies it** — model, and "supplied in Malaysia by Primaxs
   Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd, the exclusive Tanko distributor since 2006"
2. **The specification that matters** — dimensions, load rating, materials
3. **One genuine buying insight** — the thing a competitor's listing will not say
4. **The offer block** — free delivery and installation Selangor/KL, outstation
   itemised, 1-year warranty administered locally, Ringgit, 3–7 days
5. **Contact** — `Tel 03-4296 4737 | WhatsApp 011-5841 9886 | storagesystem.com.my`

Slot 1 is written and loaded as the worked example.

## The catch

**Every product submission has its own reCAPTCHA.** Twelve products means twelve
tickboxes from Wei Ming. Nothing can be done about that from this side.

**The workable rhythm:** the form is filled here in about twenty seconds, then
it needs one tick and one click. Doing four or five in a sitting is realistic;
twelve in one go is not.

Priority order is the table above — slots 1 to 4 carry the Timeline budget and
the best keywords, so if only some get done, do those.
