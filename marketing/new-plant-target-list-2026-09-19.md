# New plants under construction — target list, 19 September 2026

Private-sector leads. **No tender process, no Bumiputera preference, no price
scoring.** A facilities or project manager buys what works, which is the only
arena where your exclusivity, specification depth, Selangor stock, install crew
and local warranty are worth anything.

**The rule that governs all of this:** the buying window for workbenches, lockers,
racking and tool storage is roughly **6 to 18 months after groundbreaking**, during
fit-out. Contact them while the building is going up. Once it opens, the storage
is bought.

## What to say when you contact them

Not a catalogue. These are the four things a project or facilities manager
actually cares about, and they are all true:

1. **Exclusive Malaysia distributor for Tanko** — one accountable party, not a
   reseller chain.
2. **Stock in Selangor and a 3–7 working day delivery**, not a 6–9 week import
   lead time, during a fit-out where dates slip constantly.
3. **Your own installation crew.** They do not have to coordinate a third party.
4. **Warranty administered locally from Selangor** — nothing gets shipped back to
   Taiwan.

For NXP add ESD specifically. For Eppendorf lead with stainless and laboratory.

**Who to reach:** the project manager or facilities manager, not central
procurement. Central procurement will tell you to register on a portal. The
project manager knows what the building still needs.

## Make this a habit, not a one-off

Twenty minutes a week:

- **MIDA** media releases — `mida.gov.my/media-and-events/`
- **InvestPenang** news — `investpenang.gov.my/news/`
- **Invest Selangor**, InvestKL, and the Johor agency
- **The Edge Malaysia** and The Star business sections

Log: company, location, floor area, headcount, groundbreaking date, expected
completion. **Groundbreaking is the lead. Opening is the post-mortem.**

For scale: MIDA approved **973 manufacturing projects worth RM51.3 billion in 1H
2026** and had another 99 manufacturing proposals worth RM35.6bn under review in
August. **85% of the 5,822 projects approved since 2021 have reached
implementation** — production, construction or machinery installation. The list
refreshes continuously.
