# Review engine — the highest-value thing Primaxs is not doing

Reviews are **the number one local ranking factor** — roughly 20% of local SEO
weight, scored on quantity, velocity and sentiment — and Google now pulls review
text into AI Overviews. A competitor with twelve detailed reviews outranks a
better company with three thin ones.

## Where the profile actually stands

**Google Business Profile: 5.0 from 3 reviews.** Not zero, but close to no signal.

| Review | Problem |
|---|---|

| "storage system , warehouse products sell" | 1-review account, not a sentence |

Two of the three come from accounts with a single review and almost no text.
Google weights those close to nothing, and neither gives a buyer any reason to
choose Primaxs. **Nearly two decades of real customers exist and none of them are
visible.**

The target is **ten reviews that name a product and describe a job.** That is a
different asset entirely from ten more "nice products" reviews.

## When to ask

Timing decides the response rate far more than the wording does.

| Moment | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **2–3 days after installation** | The benefit is fresh and the install is the part people are happiest about |
| **When a customer thanks you** on WhatsApp | They have already said the nice thing; you are only asking them to repeat it where it counts |
| **After a warranty claim is resolved well** | The strongest reviews come from a problem handled properly, not from a smooth order |
| **On repeat orders** | A second order is proof of satisfaction — ask on the delivery confirmation |

Do not batch-send twenty in one day. Google reads review *velocity*, and twenty
reviews appearing in one afternoon after nineteen years of silence looks exactly
like what it looks like. **Two or three a week, steadily, is the pattern that
holds.**

## The ask — WhatsApp

The one thing that matters: **ask for specifics.** A review that says which
product and which job is worth several that say "good service".

If you have a moment, mentioning what you bought and what it replaced is
the part other buyers actually read. Takes about a minute.

Thanks,
[name] — Primaxs

## The ask — after delivery, by email

Subject: `Your Tanko order — one small favour`

```
Hi [name],

[Product] was delivered on [date] and installed on [date]. Hope it has
settled in well.

One small favour: if the equipment and the delivery were up to standard,
a short Google review would help us a lot.

  storagesystem.com.my/review

What is most useful to other buyers is the specific bit — the model, what
it is being used for, and how the delivery and installation went. Ninety
seconds is plenty.

If anything is not right, reply to this email instead and we will sort it
out first. The warranty runs a year from delivery on manufacturing defects
and it is administered from our Selangor office.

Regards,
[name]
Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd
03-4296 4737 · storagesystem.com.my
```

Note the last paragraph. It is not politeness — it routes unhappy customers to
a private reply instead of a public one-star, and it gets the problem fixed.

## Rules — do not break these

- **Never offer anything in exchange.** Discounts, vouchers, free delivery for a
  review — all against Google policy, and profiles get their reviews stripped or
  the listing suspended. There is no version of this that is safe.
- **Never write reviews from company accounts or ask staff to.** Google detects
  the pattern and it is the fastest way to lose the ones you have.
- **Never gate** ("only leave a review if you are happy"). Also a policy breach.
- **Reply to every single review**, good or bad, within a couple of days. Owner
  responses are a ranking signal in their own right and every reply is another
  chance to get the product name and the location into the profile text.

## Reply templates

**Good review, product named**
```
Thank you [name] — glad the [product] is working out. Shout if you need
extra drawer dividers or a second unit for the other bay.
— Primaxs, Balakong
```

**Good review, no detail**
```
Thanks [name], much appreciated. Anything else you need for the workshop,
the Selangor office is on 03-4296 4737.
```

**Critical review**
```
Thanks for saying so plainly, [name] — that is not the standard we want.
[What specifically went wrong] should not have happened and we are
correcting it. Please call the office on 03-4296 4737 and ask for [name];
we will make it right.
```

Never argue in a reply. A calm owner response under a bad review sells better to
the next reader than the bad review costs.
