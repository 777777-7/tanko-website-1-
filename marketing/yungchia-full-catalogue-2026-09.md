## 2. How badly I mischaracterised this company

I called it "educational and institutional furniture, mainly school desks and
chairs."

**Student desks and chairs are 32 of 253 products — 13%.**

The true shape of the business:

- **Seating of all kinds — 99 products** (chairs 51, auditorium/arena 26, beam 22)
- **Tables — 77** (conference and training 41, dining and canteen 36)
- **Steel products — 45**
- **Education — 32**

It is a **general contract and institutional furniture manufacturer**, and its
single biggest specialism is **fixed and public seating**, not schools.

## 4. Why the office line makes the plan worse, not better

**Malaysia manufactures office chairs and exports them.**

- **Oasis Furniture Industries** (Kluang, Johor, since 1998) — exports office
  furniture to **over 65 countries**
- **Classic Chair System** — **30+ years** manufacturing and exporting office chairs
- **Shah Alam and Petaling Jaya clusters** with local supply chains for **casters,
  gas lifts and upholstery**, and **BIFMA compliance**
- Plus Techno Furniture, Officepro, Vaseat, and Chinese imports and Shopee/Lazada
  volume at the budget end

**You would be importing budget institutional chairs 3,300 km into a country that
builds them locally and ships them to 65 others.** The components, the labour cost
and the certification are all already here.

**School furniture was hard because of tenders and local content. Office chairs
are harder, because Malaysia is a net exporter of them.**

**Fixed and public seating — 48 products.** Auditorium Seating ×10, **Arena

worship.

**And it is the furthest thing from your business on this whole list** —
architects, main contractors, bills of quantities, site installation during a
fit-out programme. Nothing in Primaxs touches that world.

I record it because it is the honest answer to "what are they actually best at,"
not as a recommendation.

## 9. What to ask them

One email, three questions, in this order:

1. **Does your Singapore office already cover Malaysia?** Still the question that
   decides the whole plan.
2. **What is the FOB price and container quantity for the canteen and dining
   range?** Freight per seat is the entire argument.
3. **Do you make any ESD or cleanroom seating, or would you consider it?** Cheap
   to ask, and the only version of this that fits your customers.

**Do not open with office chairs.** Malaysia exports them; leading with that
tells them you have not checked the market.

## 10. Verified vs. judgement

**Verified:** every figure in §1, §3, §5 and §6 comes from Yung Chia's own product
API — all 279 records, their categories, titles, and the individual dimension and
material specs quoted. The absence of ergonomic and BIFMA claims is a search
