# The candidate catalogues — and a correction, 17 September 2026

Wei Ming asked for the product catalogues of the Taiwanese candidates. Pulling
them turned up a field I had not used yesterday: **Taiwantrade publishes each
supplier's "Main Export Market".** That is close to the exact filter he needs,
and it changes the shortlist.

---

## 1. The filter that matters

| Company | Type | Est. | **Already exports to Malaysia?** | Products listed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **K-GUALDA MACHINE** | Manufacturer, OEM, ODM | 1985 | **NO** — US, Japan, Canada only | 7 |
| **HANTOP INTELLIGENCE** | Manufacturer, Exporter | 2018 | **NO** — but Singapore yes | 10 |
| WEY JUAN TECHNOLOGY | Manufacturer + **Online B2C retailer** | 2000 | **YES** | — |
| HOLD WELL INDUSTRIAL | Manufacturer, OEM, ODM, ISO 9001:2015 | 1983 | **YES** (~70 countries) | 10 |
| BRIGHT JING CHIN | **Exporter only — not a manufacturer** | — | **YES** (listed as all of ASIA) | — |
| GMT GLOBAL | Manufacturer, OEM, ODM | 1995 | **YES** | 69 — but linear motion and photonics, wrong category |

**A correction to yesterday's note.** I said to start with Bright Jing Chin for
pull studs. That was wrong on two counts: they are listed as an **Exporter only,
not a manufacturer**, so there is no brand to be exclusive for; and their export
market is listed as the whole of Asia, so Malaysia is not open ground. Drop them
from the shortlist.

**And the wider finding is less comfortable than yesterday's write-up implied:**
four of the six already export to Malaysia. Established Taiwanese machine-tool
accessory makers are, on the whole, *already selling into Malaysia*. The category
is better served from Taiwan than I assumed. Exclusivity here will be harder than
I made it sound.

Caveat on this filter: "exports to Malaysia" does not prove an exclusive
distributor exists. It may be direct sales, or a reseller buying ad-hoc. But it
does mean the market is not virgin, and it is the single best public signal
available before you email anyone.

## 2. The catalogues

### K-GUALDA MACHINE CO., LTD. — the only clean fit
Manufacturer / OEM / ODM · est. 1985 · Taiwan · exports: United States, Japan, Canada

Complete product list (7):

1. High speed type Live center for Lathe
2. Morse Taper MT6 / MT5 / MT4 Live center, Heavy Duty
3. Morse Taper Bull nose center
4. **CNC Collet ER32, ER20, ER16**
5. Morse Taper MT3 / MT2 ER32 / ER25 Collet Chuck
6. BT collet chuck tool holder
7. (one further collet/centre variant)

**Read:** this is lathe and turning accessories more than machining-centre
tooling. Narrow — seven products is not a business line on its own.

But **ER collets are the interesting item.** They are a true consumable: they
wear, they lose gripping accuracy, every CNC and milling shop replaces them, they
are tiny, and a set has high value per kilo. That is the best freight economics
and the best repeat-purchase profile of anything found so far. And the company
has no Asian export market listed at all, which is the strongest "open territory"
signal in the table.

### HANTOP INTELLIGENCE TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD. — high value, narrow market
Manufacturer / Exporter · est. 2018 · ~50 staff · revenue USD 1–5m · exports:
US, China, India, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Taiwan, Germany, Italy

Products (10), the relevant ones:

- Ultrasonic Tool Holder BT30-R02-06

**Read honestly:** this is ultrasonic-assisted machining for hard and brittle

This is a "grow into it" option, not a first line.

### HOLD WELL INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD. — good products, already in Malaysia

BT30 / BT40 / BT50 drill chuck holders.

### GMT GLOBAL INC. — wrong category
The shrink-fit machine that surfaced in the tool-holder search is one item out of

for you.

## 3. The method, which is worth more than the list

For any future candidate, on Taiwantrade:

1. Search the product, then open the **supplier** record, not the product.
2. Read four fields: **Type** (must say Manufacturer, not just Exporter/Trader —
   a trader has no brand to grant exclusivity for), **Year Established**,
   **Main Export Market**, **Certification**.
3. If Malaysia appears in Main Export Market, the territory is not open. Move on
   or plan to ask them directly what the existing arrangement is.
4. If the type includes **Online B2C Retailer**, they compete with their own
   distributors. Skip.

That filter takes about a minute per company and removes most of the wasted
emails.

---

## 4. Where this leaves the recommendation

Honest position after pulling the actual data:

- **K-GUALDA is the only candidate that passes every filter** — genuine
  manufacturer, 40 years old, no Asian distribution. But the range is narrow, so
  it would be a first product rather than a whole second line. ER collets are the
  item to ask about.
- **Hold Well has the best catalogue** and is worth an email anyway, simply to
  ask what their Malaysian arrangement is. If it is ad-hoc rather than exclusive,
  there may be room.
- **Hantop is a real opportunity but too early** for a first line.
- Bright Jing Chin, Wey Juan and GMT come off the list.

The broader lesson from this exercise is that six candidates gave one clean fit.
To find a genuine open territory you should expect to filter through thirty or
forty suppliers, not six. The TAITRA Trade Inquiry service is the efficient way
to do that — you state the category and the fact that you want an unrepresented
brand, and they return matched suppliers.

I have not verified prices, MOQ, quality or certification for any of these
beyond what is printed on their Taiwantrade records.
