# Online RFQ and e-sourcing platforms: where buyers post requests Primaxs can answer

Researched 3 Oct 2026. Web research only: no accounts created, no forms submitted, nothing paid.
Scope: platforms where companies or factories post RFQs. MyProcurement, TNB, Prasarana, PETRONAS, TM, Air Selangor, Lapasar, MRT Corp, Proton and Perodua were covered in earlier research and are not repeated here.

"Verified" means I read it on the platform's own page. **UNVERIFIED** means I could not confirm it there: the page blocked fetching, or the figure came from a third party.

---

## Top 5 to act on first

1. **Coupa Supplier Portal: register now (free).** Coupa's own supplier documentation says the *Sourcing > Public Sourcing Events > All Public* tab lists "all open and active events registered as Public" by **every** Coupa customer, and suppliers can take part without an invitation. No platform here has a cheaper way in. Many MNC factories run Coupa (3M, Molex and MHI each publish Coupa supplier guides). How many public events come from Malaysian buyers is **UNVERIFIED**. Log in once a week and filter by commodity.
2. **SAP Business Network Discovery: finish the profile on the account you already have.** The Prasarana Ariba self-registration already created an SAP Business Network standard account, which is free. Add the UNSPSC codes listed below and set Malaysia and Singapore as service regions. A free account can answer **unlimited public-sector postings but only 3 private-sector postings**. After that, the paid tier is the **Promote subscription at US$1,200 / SGD 1,600 a year** (SAP's support article KB0949101 lists both prices). Do not buy Promote until the leads dashboard shows real Malaysian postings.
3. **Procurehere: subscribe for RM135 a year.** Last round, the supplier price was the main thing nobody could find. Procurehere's own checkout page shows it: **"UNLIMITED BUYER Supplier Subscription – MYR 135 per annum (inclusive of SST)"**. The client list now includes industrial buyers that run workshops and plants:
   - Westports
   - Edra Power
   - Cement Industries (Sabah)
   - FGV
   - Port Klang Free Zone
   - Medivest
   - Fiberail
   - MAHB
   - IWK

   At RM135 it is worth paying just to be on the vendor lists. One catch: each event can still carry its own participation fee, and the events table has a column for it.
4. **GeBIZ (Singapore): register as a foreign GeBIZ Trading Partner. The first account is free.** You need a **Corppass account for Foreign Entities** first. Allow 2–4 weeks, and that timing is **UNVERIFIED** (it comes from a third-party blog). Opportunity headers are public. The live listing (3 Oct) shows ITE, MOE and MINDEF quotations, and there is a "Furniture, Office Equipment & Audio-Visual" category. ITE and the other TVET buyers suit workbenches. Some tenders also need the separate EPPU Government Supplier Registration, but only when the tender documents say so.
5. **MIDA business-matching days: watch for them and apply (free).** These are events, not an online board, but MNC buyers are in the room. Two examples:
   - **MIDA–Texas Instruments Malaysia Business Matching Day** (9 Jul 2025, 62 selected suppliers). Sectors sought: "E&E, Precision Engineering, Automation, Packaging, Metalworking… and other supporting industries". Registration was by Google Form.
   - **MIDA–FMM Industrial Linkage Day** (MNC anchors plus 50+ SMEs), and the **MIDA–Daikin Supply Chain Programme**.

   This route reaches E&E factories better than any online RFQ board.

**Skip:**
- **Alibaba RFQ.** Its RFQ marketplace has 163 open "tool cabinet" RFQs, of which 1 is from Malaysia and 1 from Singapore. Of 124 "workbench" RFQs, 1 is from Malaysia and none from Singapore.
- **Made-in-China and Global Sources.** Both are built around Chinese and other Asian manufacturers.
- **The export lead boards:** TradeKey, Go4WorldBusiness, EC21, ECPlaza and Tradewheel.
- **All the paid tender aggregators.** They resell MyProcurement and ePerolehan notices, and you need MOF registration to bid on those anyway.

## UNSPSC codes for SAP Discovery, Coupa and Procurehere profiles

Checked against the UNSPSC listings on usa.databasesets.com. **The two codes suggested in the brief are wrong for this product range:**
- **27112800** is "Tool attachments and accessories": saw blades, end mills, collets, tool holders.
- **56101700** is "Office furniture". Only its filing-cabinet code fits, so it covers document cabinets alone.

| Product line | Code | Official title |
|---|---|---|
| Workbenches (heavy-duty, ESD, stainless) | **56111902** | Industrial work surfaces |
| Workbenches (alt.) | **24102006** | Work benches |
| Tool cabinets, heavy-duty cabinets | **24112401** | Tool chest or cabinet |
| Tool cabinets / drawer cabinets / shelving | **56111906** | Industrial cabinets or drawers or shelving |
| Tool trolleys | **56111907** | Industrial tool carts |
| CNC tool-holder storage, mould racks, parts cabinets | **56111903** | Industrial storage units |
| Perforated boards, pegboard hooks, louvre panels, hanger racks | **56111904** | Industrial organization |
| Accessories (bins, hooks, drawer inserts) | **56111905** | Industrial parts or accessories |
| Modular workstations (power/data rails) | **56111901** | Industrial lighting or power or data components |
| Shelving / racks | **24102004** | Storage racks or shelves |
| Steel lockers | **56101520** | Lockers |
| Storage cabinets (general) | **56101530** | Storage cabinets |
| Document cabinets | **56101702** | Filing cabinets or accessories |
| Hazmat cabinets (only if Tanko range includes them) | 24112402 | Hazardous materials cabinets |

Register the whole of class **56111900 Industrial furniture** plus 24102006, 24112401, 56101520 and 56101702.

## Caveats

- **SAP's own pages refuse automated fetching.** The "3 free private-sector responses" rule is SAP's wording as it appears in search results for its pages; I could not open those pages directly. The Promote price is verified on support.ariba.com.
- **Procurehere's event tables load by script,** so I could not see which events are open today. Use the RM135 subscription, or browse each buyer's /publicEvents/<slug> page in a normal browser.
- **The Alibaba counts are open RFQs on 3 Oct 2026,** taken from the marketplace's buyer-location filter.
