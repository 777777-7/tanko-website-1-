# Institutional and large-company buyers: vendor registration routes

Researched 3 October 2026 for Primaxs Marketing (M) Sdn Bhd (Tanko exclusive distributor, Seri Kembangan).

**Facts this rests on (already in our notes):** Primaxs is **not Bumiputera** (confirmed; see
`../kod-bidang-and-action-list-2026-09-19.md`), is **not yet MOF-registered**, and the MOF codes to
register under are **020101** (furniture and lab furniture, workstations), **130101** (workshop machines and
equipment) and **060501** (lab equipment).

**Not repeated here:** MyProcurement, TNB, Prasarana, PETRONAS, TM, Procurehere (IWK, FELCRA, MAHB),
Air Selangor, MRT Corp, Proton/Perodua.

**How "verified" is used:** *Verified* means I loaded the page, or the official site linked to it, during this
session. *Verified (snippet)* means the official page would not load here (blocked, TLS error or timeout),
so the details come from a search-engine extract of that page. **UNVERIFIED** means I could not confirm it.

### Three rules that decide most rows below

1. **Value bands for a non-Bumi supplier** (from our 19 Sep note): below RM20k, buying direct needs no
   registration. **RM20k to 50k is open with MOF.** **RM50k to 100k is Bumiputera-only.** RM100k to 500k is open
   but Bumiputera bids get a price preference. Tenders above RM500k are open. Public universities run their own
   procurement rules. UTeM, for example, describes quotations as covering RM50k to RM500k (snippet), which
   implies its direct-purchase ceiling may still be RM50k. That means **being on a university's own vendor
   list matters even before you have MOF.**
2. **The MARA family (UniKL, KKTM, IKM, MRSM, likely GMI) is Bumiputera-only in practice.** A UniKL
   (Kulim) quotation for hostel furniture required MOF code 020199 **plus** a Bumiputera certificate. The way
   in is to sell goods to the Bumiputera firm that wins. That is ordinary trade and is allowed. Fronting for
   the winner, or having the contract novated to you, is not; see the Government Procurement Act 2025 note in
   the 19 Sep file.
3. **DBKL quotations require a Wilayah Persekutuan KL address.** Notice 29/2026 required "Bumiputera
   registered with KKM, with an address in WP Kuala Lumpur only". Primaxs, in Selangor, is out of DBKL
   quotations.

## Notes

- **Monitoring tool:** TenderPanel (https://www.tenderpanel.com) aggregates Malaysian government tenders and
  quotations with daily keyword email alerts on its Basic and Pro plans. I did not check prices. It is useful
  for watching "meja kerja", "workbench", "kabinet alatan", "loker" and "peralatan bengkel" without logging
  into each portal.
- **TCC "PATUH":** a Tax Compliance Certificate showing compliant status. USM, UiTM, DBKL and UMPSA notices all
  require one. Get it from LHDN alongside MOF.
- **Government procurement value bands:** the MOF page I loaded (jenis-dan-cara-perolehan-kerajaan.pdf, dated
  2016) still shows direct purchase up to RM50k. Our 19 Sep note uses the newer RM20k split. Use the newer one
  for federal agencies, and check each university's own threshold.
