Researched 3 October 2026, from web search, fetched pages, and direct `curl` checks of
competitor sites and platform help pages. **UNVERIFIED** means I couldn't confirm it from
a primary source: either the page blocked bots, the figure was only in an image, or only
an aggregator reported it.

Not repeated here because they're already done: GBP, Bing Places, FB Page and groups,
LinkedIn, NEWPAGES, B2BMap, Lapasar, Yellow Pages, InfoPages, MalaysiaListings,
BeOnline, Seek Business, all.biz and interepo.

**About heavy items on marketplaces:** a 100–300 kg Tanko cabinet can't go through any
platform courier. Shopee and Lazada only work for these if you're approved for own-fleet or
Delivered-by-Seller delivery and limit them to Selangor/KL, which is your free-delivery
area anyway. Outstation orders would need a quoted freight line. List those as "contact
for quote", or don't list them outside the Klang Valley.
