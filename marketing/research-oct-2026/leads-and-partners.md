## Grants and incentives note

**Plain answer: no current Malaysian grant pays for workbenches, tool cabinets or
storage as such.** The grants are for automation and digitalisation, and furniture
is not automation. Do not tell a customer "this is grant-claimable" — it will
backfire at audit. What genuinely exists:

| Scheme | Covers workshop furniture? | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Small value asset (SVA) capital allowance** (Income Tax Act Sch. 3; Public Ruling 3/2021) | **YES — the one real lever** | Each asset costing **≤ RM2,000** gets **100% capital allowance in the year bought**. Non-SMEs capped at RM20,000 a year in total; **SMEs have no cap**. Many single Tanko benches, cabinets and trolleys sit under RM2,000, so an SME can write the whole order off in one year.
