## The headline discovery

**Every competitor ranking above us has a NEWPAGES microsite on its own `.n.my`
subdomain. We do not.**

Verified indexed in Google today:

| Subdomain | Company | Where they rank |
|---|---|---|
| `machinerytools.n.my` | Sui U Machinery & Tools | **#3** for "tool cabinet malaysia" |
| `knightauto.n.my` | Knight Auto | **#1** |
| `sptools.n.my` | SP Tools | **#7** |
| `mksteel.n.my` | MK Steel Hardware | — |
| `e-rack.n.my` | E-Rack | — |
| `multiplussteelworks.n.my` | Multi Plus Steel Works | — |
| **`primaxs.n.my`** | **us** | **does not exist** |

### Why it matters — verified on `machinerytools.n.my`
It is a **full separate microsite**, indexed by Google under its own domain, and
it links out to the company's real website:

```
<a href="http://www.machinerytools.com.my/" target="_blank">Visit Official Website</a>
   rel = (none)   ->  FOLLOWED
<a href="https://www.suiumachinery.com.my/">New Website</a>
   rel = (none)   ->  FOLLOWED
```

Checked every outbound link on that page: **not one carries `rel="nofollow"`.**
They also link Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok the same way.

So the NEWPAGES paid tier is not a directory listing. It is a second indexed
site that passes link equity to your main domain. That is precisely the
mechanism we lack, and it explains how NEWPAGES-built competitors hold #1, #3
and #7 for our biggest head term.

**Action taken:** emailed `sales@newpages.com.my` 13 Sep naming those five
subdomains and asking exactly which plan includes it, what it costs per year,
whether 1,700 SKUs can be imported rather than keyed, and whether the microsite
can carry a followed link to `www.storagesystem.com.my`.

**This is the one paid item on this page worth serious consideration.**

contact domain weaken the local trust signal that feeds the map pack — which is
where Malaysian B2B buyers actually click.

## Marketplace gap
Knight Auto runs a full Shopee store, which ranks on its own. A search for Tanko
products across Shopee and Lazada returns **three listings**, none of them ours.

Whether to sell on a marketplace is a commercial decision, not an SEO one —
marketplace fees and public pricing both matter. Flagging it, not pushing it.

---

## Deliberately NOT recommended

**Mass directory submission.** It does not move a site from position 43 to 1,
and Google discounts or penalises low-quality link networks. Two excellent links
beat two hundred poor ones. The links that count here are: the manufacturer
(Tanko), a NEWPAGES microsite, a claimed Yelp listing, and real customers.
