Everything below is measured from Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, or a
live check. Where I am inferring, I say so.

## 2. The pattern in our own query data

This is the whole story in six rows:

| Query | Impressions | Clicks | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| tool cabinet | 125 | **0** | 0% |
| tool cabinet malaysia | 124 | **0** | 0% |
| warehouse storage malaysia | 97 | **0** | 0% |
| heavy duty tools cabinet | 80 | **0** | 0% |
| tool display board | 10 | **3** | **30%** |
| perforated board | 20 | **2** | **10%** |

**Head terms give us impressions and nothing else. Long-tail terms convert at
10–30%.** We are being *shown* on the big terms — 426 impressions across those
four alone — and never clicked, because at position ~43 nobody scrolls that far.

The lesson is not "chase the head terms harder". It is that our impressions on
them are already there, and converting them needs rank, which needs links. Our
own clicks come from specific, low-volume phrases where we already rank.

## 3. The AI-visibility gap, demonstrated rather than asserted

I asked an AI search engine for the best industrial workbench and tool cabinet
suppliers in Malaysia. The answer named:

1. Knight Auto Sdn Bhd
2. Soon Rex
3. Vertex's Engineering Works
4. Alliance Supplies Sdn Bhd
5. RS Malaysia

**Primaxs was absent.** That is the concrete version of "customers ask AI and
don't find us".

Worth noting *what it cited*: the suppliers' own product pages — not
third-party listicles. So this is winnable with our own pages, not dependent on
getting into someone else's article.

### What the cited pages have in common

Look at the Knight Auto page title the AI pulled:

> Cabinet Tool Cart Tool Storage & Tool Boxes **Selangor, Malaysia, Kuala Lumpur
> (KL), Seri Kembangan, Setapak, Kajang** Supplier, Suppliers, Supply, Supplies
> | Knight Auto Sdn Bhd

Alliance Supplies uses the identical pattern. Ours:

> Tool Cabinets & Trolleys Malaysia — from RM1,025 | Primaxs

Theirs is keyword stuffing and it reads badly. I am **not** recommending we copy
it — Google's spam policies cover exactly that, and it would cheapen the site.
But it tells us something real: **the pages being cited name the places they
serve, and ours name one country.**

The legitimate version of that is the one we already started and never
finished: we have six location pages (`ipoh-perak`, `johor`,
`kuala-lumpur-selangor`, `kuching-sarawak`, `melaka`, `penang`). Seri Kembangan,
Shah Alam, Klang, Puchong, Rawang and Balakong — the actual industrial belt we
deliver to free — have none.

## 4. What to do, in order of return

### A. Links — still the binding constraint
Two referring domains against 10 and 11. Nothing on-page fixes position 43 for
"tool cabinet malaysia". Covered in `why-competitors-outrank-us-evidence.md`;
the NEWPAGES tier is the lever and it costs money.

### B. Location pages for the Klang Valley industrial belt — free, ours to do
Six exist; the ones that matter most are missing. A real page for Shah Alam,
Klang, Puchong, Seri Kembangan and Balakong — each naming the industrial parks,
the free-delivery promise, and the ranges most bought there — is legitimate,
useful to a buyer, and puts the place names on pages that can be cited.

### C. Answer-shaped content for AI extraction
The research is consistent that assistants lift **declarative, factual
sentences** almost verbatim. Our guides are written as arguments; our spec
tables are already ideal. What is missing is a short, factual answer block at
the top of each range page — "Tanko tool cabinets carry to 400kg, drawers 100kg
standard and 200kg T-drawer, central locking across the stack, stocked in
Selangor" — the kind of sentence an assistant can quote whole.

### D. Fix the head-term CTR problem we *can* fix
On queries where we already rank in the top 10 (perforated board, tool display
board, hanger rack, parts bin, document cabinet), CTR is 10–30%. Those are worth
protecting and extending with more specific pages, because they are where our
actual traffic comes from.

### E. Keep the free indexing pressure on
Bing URL Submission is **100 URLs/day** and resets daily — that is 700 a week
for free while Bing holds 28 of our pages. IndexNow takes all 1,847 per push.

## 5. What I checked and rejected

- **The "free Malaysia directory" listicles.** They recommend B2BMap. I checked
  B2BMap directly: it carries **no external links on any listing at any tier**.
  Being listed there does nothing for rank.
- **robuta.com** — a private search engine, not a directory.
- **onesync.my** — NEWPAGES' own product, not an independent source.
- **MyPages** — add-listing page is broken (raw shortcode).
- **JawatanKosong** — subscription only, no plans currently available.
- **MATRADE / SME Corp** — exporter and SME-registry directories. Primaxs is a
  distributor and importer, so eligibility is doubtful; worth one phone call
  before spending time.
