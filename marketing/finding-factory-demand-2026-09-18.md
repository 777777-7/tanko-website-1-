# Finding factory requirements directly — 18 September 2026

Wei Ming's question: dealers bring him factory requirements, several dealers quote
the same job, and the margin gets competed away. Is there a portal where factory
requirements are published, so he can go to the factory directly?

**Short answer: the portal you are imagining mostly does not exist for this
product — but the problem you described has a standard fix that costs nothing,
and there is a much better demand signal than a tender feed.**

---

## 1. Name the actual problem first

You are the **exclusive Malaysia distributor for Tanko**. So when three dealers
chase the same factory with Tanko product, all three are buying from you.

That means:

- You quote the same job three times.
- The three dealers undercut each other to win it.
- **Tanko's street price in Malaysia falls**, and it falls because of your own
  channel, not because of a competitor.
- You still make the sale, but at dealer price on a job that has been beaten
  down — and you never meet the factory.

The instinct is to go direct. Before that, there is a cheaper fix.

---

## 2. Deal registration — the fix that needs no portal

This is standard practice in IT, electronics and industrial distribution, and the
research describes your exact situation:

> *A distributor sees an opportunity with an end customer, but the deal will be
> fulfilled by a dealer that buys from the distributor. The dealer wants
> protection from other dealers also pursuing the same end customer.*

**How it works.** The first dealer to register an end-user and project with you
gets protected pricing on that project for a fixed window — typically 60 or 90
days, renewable if they show it is still live. Any other dealer asking you to
quote the same end-user and project gets list price, not project price.

**Why it works.** It removes the reason for your dealers to undercut each other.
The registered dealer knows their margin is safe, so they sell on specification
and service instead of dropping price. Your product's price in the market stops
sliding.

**Why you can do it.** You hold the exclusive distributorship. You set the terms.
No platform, no fee, no permission needed — it is a one-page policy and a
spreadsheet.

**What it costs you.** Some dealers will grumble. The ones who grumble loudest
are usually the ones who were winning on price rather than effort.

**This is the single highest-return action on this page, and it is free.**

---

## 3. Going direct — the honest trade-off

Going direct to a factory that a dealer introduced you to is how you win one job
and lose a dealer who would have brought ten. Dealers talk to each other.

Direct makes sense where **no dealer has a relationship to damage**:

- Factories that have never appeared in any dealer's quote request
- **New plants that do not exist yet** — see below, this is the real opportunity
- Government and institutional buyers, which most of your dealers ignore

Keep a simple rule: if the name is already in your quote history against a
dealer, it is that dealer's account. If it is not, it is open.

---

## 4. Where factory requirements actually surface

### a) The best signal by a distance: new factories being built

This is where I would put the effort. **A plant that is being built has not bought
its storage yet.** Once it opens, it has.

Real, current examples from MIDA and InvestPenang announcements:

| Project | Scale | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| **MKS Instruments**, Penang | 17 acres, ~500,000 sq ft, **~1,000 employees**, RM400m+ | 1,000 staff means a locker requirement in the hundreds, plus tool rooms and maintenance benches |
| **Winner Sky Technology**, Batu Kawan | 60,000 sq ft, opened June 2026 | Already opened — too late, and that is the lesson |
| **SkyGate NHJ Technology** | 36,000 sq ft smart manufacturing, Sept 2026 | Also just opened |
| **Benchmark**, Penang | 5th facility | Repeat builder, worth a standing relationship |

**The timing is the whole thing.** The buying window for workbenches, lockers,
racking and tool storage is roughly **6 to 18 months after groundbreaking**, during
fit-out. Winner Sky and SkyGate opening this year means their storage was ordered
months ago. **Groundbreaking announcements are the lead. Opening announcements are
the post-mortem.**

**Where these are published, free:**

- **MIDA** media releases and announcements — `mida.gov.my/media-and-events/`
- **InvestPenang** news — `investpenang.gov.my/news/`
- **Invest Selangor**, InvestKL and the other state investment agencies
- **The Edge Malaysia** and The Star business sections carry them too

MIDA approved **973 manufacturing projects worth RM51.3 billion in 1H 2026**, and
as of August was reviewing another 99 manufacturing proposals worth RM35.6bn.
**85% of the 5,822 projects approved since 2021 have reached implementation —
production, factory construction or machinery installation.** That is a large,
continuously refreshed list of buildings being fitted out.

**This is a weekly habit, not a project.** Read the announcements, note the ones
in Selangor, Penang and Johor with real floor area or headcount, and write to the
facilities or project manager while the building is still going up.

### b) ePerolehan — a real portal, and you are ignoring it

Government and GLC buyers **do** publish requirements, on **ePerolehan**
(transactions and supplier registration) and **MyProcurement** (public tender
notices).

You sell lockers, workbenches, lab furniture and racking. The buyers for those in
the public sector are **schools, polytechnics, universities, teaching hospitals,
technical training centres and government workshops** — and they buy by tender.

**MOF registration costs RM450 for three years.** That is the cheapest access to
published requirements available to you, and most of your dealers are not there.

Note the 2026 change: the Government Procurement Act 2025 is gazetted and
introduces a **novation offence aimed at the "fronting" model** — registering and
then passing the job to someone else. If you go this route, quote and deliver it
yourself.

### c) Approved vendor lists — be on the list before the need exists

Most MNC plants buy indirect and MRO spend from an **approved vendor list**. By
the time a requirement is written, the shortlist is already decided. Registration
routes vary — some use their own portal, some use **SAP Ariba** or **Coupa**, some
use Zycus.

There is no shortcut and no aggregator. It is a per-company application, and it is
slow. **Do it for the ten plants you most want, not for everyone.**

### d) Lapasar — a genuine channel, with a catch

**Lapasar** is the most substantial Malaysian B2B procurement platform found. It is
**not a directory** — it is a reseller with its own infrastructure: three
Peninsular distribution centres, 250,000 sq ft of warehousing, 100+ trucks,
10,000+ verified suppliers, 100+ corporate clients including GLCs and
multinationals. It explicitly lists **"MRO & Industrial Supplies"** and serves
manufacturing, oil and gas, healthcare, education and the public sector. There is
a "Become a Vendor" route.

**The catch is the obvious one: they are another middle layer.** Supplying Lapasar
is supplying a reseller — the same margin structure you are trying to escape,
under a different name. It is worth an application as a volume channel, not as a
solution to the problem you asked about.

Others in the same space: **Buy2M** (post an RFQ, receive competing quotes),
**Made in Malaysia**, TradeFord. Thinner, but free to list.

### e) FMM — a target list, not a feed

The Federation of Malaysian Manufacturers directory is a **list of manufacturers**,
not a list of requirements. Useful for building the prospect list in (a) and (c).
Membership is RM500 entrance plus annual.

---

## 5. The thing you should know before you spend time on this

**Industrial storage is almost never tendered on its own in the private sector.**
It is either:

- bought by the **facilities or maintenance manager** on a purchase order, from
  whoever is on the approved list; or
- **bundled into a fit-out contract** and bought by the main contractor, in which
  case your customer is the contractor, not the factory.

So there is no feed where a factory posts *"we need 200 workbenches"*. The
equivalent of that feed is: **know the plant is being built, and already be on the
vendor list when the facilities manager starts asking.**

That is unglamorous and it works. A portal would be easier, which is exactly why
everyone would already be using it.

---

## 6. What I would do, in order

1. **Write the deal registration policy this week.** One page. It fixes the
   problem you actually described, costs nothing, and does not risk a single
   dealer relationship.
2. **Set up a weekly 20-minute scan** of MIDA and InvestPenang announcements for
   new plants in Selangor, Penang and Johor. Log company, location, floor area,
   headcount, and expected completion.
3. **Register with MOF / ePerolehan — RM450 for 3 years.** Institutional lockers
   and lab furniture are a real market your dealers are not covering.
4. **Pick ten target plants and start vendor registration.** Slow, so start now.
5. **Apply to Lapasar** as a volume channel, eyes open about the margin.
6. **Leave the main contractors until last** — they are the hardest buyer and the
   most price-driven, but on a fit-out they hold the order.

# VERIFIED LIVE IN YOUR BROWSER — same day

Wei Ming pointed out my search tool is US-geolocated, so I redid the key checks
through his Chrome on Malaysian results. This section is what I saw with my own
eyes, not inference.

## MyProcurement is open, public and free

`myprocurement.treasury.gov.my` loads without a login. Anyone can read the
advertisements. Its sections:

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| **PERANCANGAN PEROLEHAN** | **Agencies publish what they plan to buy, before the tender exists.** This is the early-warning feed and almost nobody works it |
| IKLAN | Live adverts — Sebut Harga, Tender, Requisition |
| KEPUTUSAN | Results, including who won and therefore who your competitors are |
| ARKIB | Archive |

Registration on ePerolehan (MOF, RM450 / 3 years) is needed to *bid*. It is not
needed to *read*. **You can start reading tonight for nothing.**

The portal is busy: 8,008 visitors the day I looked, 114,050 that month, 932,797
since January 2026.

## Live and recent items that are literally your product

Pulled from MyProcurement's own front page and from Malaysian search results:

- **"CADANGAN KERJA-KERJA MEMBINA DAN MENYIAPKAN BENGKEL BAGI PROGRAM DIPLOMA
  TEKNOLOGI AUTOMOTIF DI TVET…"** — building out an **automotive workshop for a
  TVET diploma programme**. A teaching workshop needs benches, tool cabinets,
  tool storage and lockers. This is the clearest example of the pattern.

**So the answer to "is there a portal where requirements are posted" is yes — for
the public sector. It has been there all along and your dealers are not in it.**
