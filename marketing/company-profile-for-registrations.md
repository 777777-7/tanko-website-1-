## Still missing — you will be asked for these

I do not hold, and could not find on the site:

- **Paid-up capital** (PETRONAS scales its licence upgrade fee on this)
- **Bank account details** for payment setup
- **Director names and IC numbers**
- **SST registration number**, if registered
- **MOF certificate number** — you do not have one yet; it gates TNB
- **CIDB grade**, if any
- Scanned **SSM documents** (Form 9 / 24 / 49, or the Super Form)

Put these in this file once and every future registration takes ten minutes.

## Category wording that works

When a form asks what you supply, use words their taxonomy already contains —
**FURNITURE**, **HARDWARE**, **OFFICE EQUIPMENT**, **GENERAL SUPPLIES**,
**WORKSHOP EQUIPMENT**, **SAFETY & STORAGE**. Those are the literal category tags
IWK uses on Procurehere, so they are what buyers filter on.
