# New plants and expansions — researched 20 September 2026

Sourced from **MIDA's own media release archive**, not from a directory. These are
announcements the companies made themselves, with dates.

**The rule that ranks this list:** the buying window for workbenches, tool storage,
lockers and racking is roughly **6 to 18 months after groundbreaking**, during
fit-out. A plant that has already opened has bought its storage.

---

## 1. BAC — Baltimore Aircoil Company, Nilai, Negeri Sembilan

**The best lead on this page.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Announced | **Groundbreaking 5 August 2026** — six weeks ago |
| Investment | **US$150 million** |
| Site | **7.36 hectares**, Nilai, Negeri Sembilan |
| Phase 1 | **30,000 sq m** manufacturing plant plus a regional engineering office |
| Phase 1 completion | **Late 2027** |
| Full completion | 2030 |
| Products | Closed-circuit cooling towers, adiabatic coolers, liquid cooling |

**Why this one matters more than the others:**

- **The timing is exactly right.** Ground broken six weeks ago, Phase 1 finishing
  late 2027. That puts fit-out and equipment installation through **2027** — you are
  early, which is where you want to be.
- **Cooling towers are heavy metal fabrication.** That means a real workshop:
  workbenches, heavy duty tool cabinets, trolleys, parts storage. Not an office
  furniture job.
- **A regional engineering office** on top of the plant means workstations and
  storage of a different kind again.
- **Nilai is roughly 40 minutes from Seri Kembangan**, and it is the same state
  where Primaxs was incorporated. Local supplier, not outstation.
- **It runs to 2030.** This is not one order, it is a multi-year fit-out.

**Action: get on the vendor list during 2026, not when the building is finished.**

## 2. Mondelēz International — Shah Alam, Selangor

| | |
|---|---|
| Announced | **11 September 2026** — nine days ago |
| Investment | **RM90 million**, their single largest site investment |
| Site | **Cadbury Confectionery Plant, Shah Alam** — operating since 1974 |
| What | A "Crumb Tower" for in-house cocoa crumb and liquid chocolate production |
| Why | Removes the need to import crumb from Australia or South Africa |

**Why it fits you specifically:**

- **Food manufacturing means stainless steel.** This is the part of the Tanko range
  a generic local fabricator cannot match — **304-grade, 1.2 mm, across 52 product
  lines**. Wash-down areas, food contact surfaces.
- **Shah Alam is inside your free delivery and free installation zone.** That is a
  genuine commercial argument here, not a talking point.
- An existing plant expanding on-site still fits out the new building.

**Note honestly:** Tanko does **not** supply 316-grade stainless. If their
specification calls for 316, say so early rather than discover it at quotation.

## The context number

**Malaysia secured RM218.5 billion in approved investments in 1H 2026, with
domestic investment in manufacturing up 23%** (MIDA, 28 August 2026).

That is the pipeline behind this list. It refreshes continuously, which is why the

## How to keep this current — 20 minutes a week

`mida.gov.my/media-release/` has a filter by year and month. Read the new entries,
log any groundbreaking or new facility, and note the state and the product. Anything
metal, mechanical, electronics, laboratory or food goes on your list.

**Groundbreaking is the lead. Opening is the post-mortem.**

# Second research pass — more names, and one date correction

## Correction first: SPIL is not a new lead

Search results surface **Siliconware (SPIL) breaking ground on an RM6 billion
semiconductor packaging plant in Penang** and it reads like the biggest opportunity
on any list. **It broke ground on 24 May 2024** — over two years ago. The P1 plant
is likely operational or close to it.

**Its storage was bought long ago.** Do not chase it. This is exactly why every
lead needs its groundbreaking date checked before any effort goes into it.
