## What follows from this

**Do not** copy the perforated-board page onto /tool-cabinet/ and expect to beat
Knight Auto. Position 43.6 against a site that owns #1 and #2 is an authority
problem, and authority is backlinks, and backlinks take months.

**Do** make every mother page own its whole cluster of trade names. A buyer
searching "tool display board" and a buyer searching "perforated board" want the
same product; the site currently only speaks one of those languages. These terms
already rank between 4 and 20 with no page addressing them directly.

That is the fast track. The slow track is [`backlink-targets.md`](backlink-targets.md).
