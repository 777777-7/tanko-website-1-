## Read this first — why "a bunch" is the wrong target

**storagesystem.com.my was parked until September 2026.** The domain is 19 years
old on paper and roughly three weeks old to Google. That changes the prescription
completely.

A three-week-old domain that suddenly acquires dozens of low-quality directory
links has the exact footprint of a link scheme. It is the one pattern most likely
to get a new site filtered rather than ranked. **Volume is not what is missing
here — relevance is.**

What is missing is a handful of links from places that are *about industrial
storage in Malaysia*. I found four of those today, and one of them is free,
already half-built, and being denied to you while every other Tanko distributor
in the region has it.

So: this page has a small Tier 1 that is worth real effort, a Tier 2 of mentions
you already have that carry no link, and a Tier 3 of general directories that are
fine to do but will not move rankings on their own. **I would rather you did the
six things in Tier 1 and 2 than sixty in Tier 3.**
