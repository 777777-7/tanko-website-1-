## The link question, answered properly

Wei Ming asked why posts were going out without a link to the website, since
traffic is the whole point. Fair challenge. The earlier "never link" rule was
too blunt. Here is the corrected policy.

### What each platform actually does to a link

| Platform | Link penalty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | **None** | The CTA button is a native, first-class link. Google *wants* the click. |
| **Facebook Page** | Real — external links are "leaks" from Meta's ecosystem | First-comment workaround still works on Facebook |
| **LinkedIn** | ~60% of reach | And the first-comment workaround is **also penalised** as of early 2026 |

### The rule

**Put the link where the platform does not tax it, and where the click is
genuinely the point.**

- **GBP → always link.** Every post gets a CTA button pointed at the relevant category page, never the homepage. This is the traffic engine right now and it costs nothing.
- **Facebook Page → link in the body, for now.** See the caveat below.
- **LinkedIn → no link in the body by default.** Roughly one post in four carries one deliberately, accepting the reach cost.

### The caveat that actually matters more than any of this

**The link penalty only bites when you have an audience to lose.** LinkedIn has
4 connections. The Facebook Page has almost no followers. Sixty percent of a
very small number is still a very small number.

So at this stage the priority order is:

1. **Look legitimate.** A Page with nothing on it fails the procurement check described in section 3 of the research doc. Posting frequently, with links, matters more than optimising reach on an audience that does not exist yet.
2. **Build the audience.** LinkedIn connections, Facebook group membership.
3. **Then** start optimising reach — switch Facebook to link-in-first-comment once the Page passes roughly 500 followers.

**And the honest ranking of what drives traffic to storagesystem.com.my today:**
organic search and Google Business Profile are doing nearly all of it. Social is
building the credibility layer that makes procurement say yes once search has
already brought them in. Treating LinkedIn as the traffic source would be the
wrong read.

the one actively building a network. Its posts are what make invitees accept.

Times are MYT. 09:00–10:00 was chosen because Malaysian factory and workshop
decision-makers are at their desks before the floor gets busy.

## Facebook groups — the actual automotive channel

Page reach is 1–3% of followers. Group reach is not. Malaysian workshop owners,
hardware retailers and machinery traders are genuinely active in groups, and
this is the shortest path to the highest-volume segment in the research.

**Rules, because getting this wrong gets you banned and the Page reported:**

1. **Read the group rules first.** Most industrial and trading groups ban vendor posts outright, or restrict them to a specific day.
2. **Never sell on the first post.** Answer somebody else's question first. Two or three useful answers before anything of your own.
3. **No link on a first post in any group.** The link goes in a comment, only when someone asks.
4. **One group at a time.** The same post copy-pasted into eight groups within an hour is the exact pattern Facebook flags as spam.
5. **Post as the Page only where the group allows it.** Many groups only accept posts from personal profiles.

**Target groups:**

| Group | Segment | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tools & Garage Equipment Trading - Malaysia | Automotive | Direct fit — tool cabinets, trolleys, benches |
| Heavy Equipment Machinery Malaysia | Plant, machinery | Depot and workshop storage |
| Bengkel / workshop diagnostic groups | Automotive | Where the bengkel owners actually are |
| Malaysian manufacturing and factory groups | Manufacturing | Benches, racking, lockers |
| Hardware shop / industrial supply groups | Retail resale | Potential dealers, not just end users |

**The useful-answer content to lead with** (no selling, no link):

- The 15-minutes-per-tool-search arithmetic
- Why cheap drawer slides bind under load
- Locker ventilation in Malaysian humidity
- Bench height by task
- Whether the sag in a year-old bench is the frame or the top
