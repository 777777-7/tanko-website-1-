## Loads, but wrong channel — a judgement, not a broken link (3)

| Portal | URL |
|---|---|
| Intel | `supplier.intel.com` |
| Infineon | `infineon.com/cms/en/about-infineon/company/procurement/` |
| Flex | `flex.com/suppliers` |

Global supplier portals exist for **direct materials and strategic categories**.
Workbenches, lockers and tool cabinets are **indirect / MRO spend**, bought locally
by the facilities or site team on a local purchase order. Registering here to sell
tool cabinets to a Penang plant is filing paperwork into a system that is not
looking for you. **Reach the facilities manager instead** — same rule as the
new-plant list.
