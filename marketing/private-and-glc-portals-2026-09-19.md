# Portals beyond government — researched 19 September 2026

Every URL below was opened and read today, not recalled. Dead ends are listed too,
so you do not waste an afternoon on them.

## First: this reverses part of what I told you this morning

I said **skip the RM450 MOF registration** because the Sebut Harga feed was thin
for your product. That was right about the *government* feed.

**It was incomplete.** TNB's supplier requirements page says, in plain text:

> **Registration requirements — Supply of Materials and Services:**
> *Valid certification with the Ministry of Finance*

**MOF registration is the gate to TNB, not just to government tenders.** The RM450
does not only buy you access to 199 thin Sebut Harga listings — it is the
prerequisite for becoming a registered supplier to the national utility, which
runs depots, substations and maintenance workshops in every state.

**That makes the RM450 worth paying.** Not for the reason we were testing this
morning, but for a better one. The government tender feed is the weak argument;
GLC vendor registration is the strong one.

## Tier 1 — register on these

### 1. TNB — Tenaga Nasional

| | |
|---|---|
| **Register** | **https://srm.tnb.com.my/ros** (Supply Chain Management System) |
| Requires | **Valid MOF certificate** for Supply of Materials and Services |
| Fee | **RM50 per submission** — covers new registration, renewal and any later amendment |
| Live tenders | `tnb.com.my/doing-business-with-tnb/suppliers/tender-notice` |

**Why it fits you:** TNB runs maintenance depots and stores statewide. Workbenches,
tool cabinets, lockers and racking are exactly what those consume.

**Do MOF first.** Without the MOF certificate the TNB registration cannot complete.
That is the dependency that decides your order of operations.

### 2. Prasarana — Rapid Bus and Rapid Rail

| | |
|---|---|
| **Self-register (free)** | **https://s1.ariba.com/Sourcing/Main/ad/selfRegistration?realm=prasarana** |
| Supplier portal | `prasarana.supplier.ariba.com` |
| Existing vendors | email `vendorregistration@prasarana.com.my` |
| MOF required? | **Not stated.** No MOF or CIDB requirement appears on the registration notice |

**This is the best first move on the page: it is free, it is self-service, and it
does not appear to require MOF.**

**Why it fits you unusually well:** Prasarana operates **bus and rail depots** —
Subang Depot, Kelana Jaya Line, Ampang Line, Rapid Bus across Central and Northern
regions. A depot is a maintenance workshop at scale. Their current live tender list
includes depot fencing, bus tyre supply and servicing, and diesel exhaust fluid —
which tells you they buy consumables and workshop items through this same channel,
not only megaprojects.

They run on **SAP Ariba**, so one registration also puts you on the Ariba Network.

### 3. PETRONAS

| | |
|---|---|
| **Portal** | **https://licensing.petronas.com/vendor/login** (PLMS) |
| Overview | `petronas.com/suppliers/licensing-procurement-malaysia` |
| Procurement platform | GEP SMART (they have moved off the old SUS system) |
| Processing time | **~10 working days** for company and SWEC application |

**Take the Registration path, not the License path.** Their own FAQ (effective May
2026) draws the line clearly:

> *A License is required for vendors that intend to provide products and/or
> services to the Upstream and/or Downstream sector of the oil and gas industry in
> Malaysia. Registration is for vendors that intend to supply products and/or
> services to the Downstream sector within the PETRONAS Group of Companies.*

You are supplying storage furniture to their facilities, not performing oil and gas
work. **Registration is the lighter path and the correct one.** You can upgrade to
a License later; the upgrade fee scales with paid-up capital.

**Why it fits you:** refineries and petrochemical plants have large tool rooms —
and they are the most likely buyer in Malaysia for the **flammable and chemical
storage cabinets** we saw UiTM tendering for. If the Chung Fu line ever happens,
this registration is what makes it sellable.

### And a judgement call, not a broken link

**Global semiconductor and EMS supplier portals — Intel, Infineon, Flex — are the
wrong channel for you, even though they load.** Those portals exist for direct
materials and strategic categories. Workbenches, lockers and tool cabinets are
*indirect* or MRO spend, and at a Malaysian plant that is bought locally by the
facilities or site team, often on a local purchase order.

**This is the same conclusion as the NXP note: go to the project or facilities
manager, not to central procurement.** Registering on Intel's global supplier
portal to sell tool cabinets to their Penang site would be filing paperwork into a
system that is not looking for you.

## The order I would do it in

1. **Prasarana Ariba self-registration** — free, self-service, no MOF gate, and
   depots are a genuine fit. Do this first because nothing blocks it.
2. **MOF registration, RM450** — now justified, because it unlocks TNB.
3. **TNB registration, RM50** — immediately after MOF clears.
4. **PETRONAS PLMS Registration** — ~10 working days, and it is the one that makes
   the flammable cabinet line sellable later.

## What I did not verify

- Whether Prasarana's registration *form* asks for MOF even though the public
  notice does not say so. You will find out at step 1 of the form.
- Whether PETRONAS Registration (as opposed to License) carries a fee. The FAQ
  states the upgrade fee scales with paid-up capital but does not price the base
  registration.
- Whether any of these four have a Bumiputera-status field that affects eligibility
  rather than just reporting. Government procurement does; GLC vendor registration
  may differ.
- MAHB's real procurement URL. Their public site does not expose one that works;
  it may sit behind a portal that is not linked from the main site.
