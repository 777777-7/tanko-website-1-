## One honest caveat about these images

At 1440px the model codes are sharp. **In a Facebook mobile feed the poster is
rendered about 500px wide** — six product columns across, so each code lands at
roughly 80px and will not be legible until someone taps to zoom.

That is acceptable for a *range overview* post, whose job is "look how much of
this exists" rather than "read this code". It is the wrong format for pushing
one product. Do not reuse these as single-product creatives — a single product
photo will always outperform a grid there.

---

## Posting rhythm — this matters

**Do not post all four to the Page on one day.** Two constraints, both learned
the hard way on this account:

1. **The Page throttle.** A second Page post with group cross-posting inside an
   hour silently fails — it looks published, but nothing appears. Retrying that
   pattern is what gets Pages restricted.
2. **Four catalogue grids in a row reads as a dump**, not as a supplier worth
   following.

```
Day 1   Facebook post 1  (machine shops)      + share to groups
Day 2   LinkedIn         (single post, below)
Day 4   Facebook post 2  (warehouse / 5S)     + share to groups
Day 7   Facebook post 3  (assembly & moulding)
Day 10  Facebook post 4  (workbenches)
```

**Facebook identity: the Primaxs Marketing Page only — never the Wei Ming Wong
personal profile.**
